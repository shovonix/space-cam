Connect your camera

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a Mac.
  2. On the camera: MENU → Network → Ctrl w/ Smartphone → Off.
  3. MENU → Setup → USB Connection → PC Remote.
  4. Quit Imaging Edge, Photos and Image Capture, then plug in the USB‑C cable.

Built and tested on Sony A7 III. Other cameras may work through gPhoto2.

If the camera won't connect

Good to know

  • Only one tab or app can hold the camera at a time. Disconnect here before using it elsewhere.
  • The A7 III stops a clip at 29:59. Space Cam warns you from 29:00.
  • The preview is for framing (about 1024×576). Recording quality is set on the camera.
